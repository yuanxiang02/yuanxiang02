## Research highlight

### VDE Bench · Visual Document Editing

A bilingual benchmark with **3,506 instruction-guided editing examples** for Chinese and English document images. Evaluation covers text correctness, structural fidelity, and preservation of content outside the edited region.

Included in the [Hugging Face official benchmark directory](https://huggingface.co/datasets?benchmark=benchmark%3Aofficial) as of November 2026.

[Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00122) · [Code](https://github.com/zion-zion-zion/VDE-Bench) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hongzhuyi/VDEBench)
